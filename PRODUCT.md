# PRODUCT

**Exora** — a glanceable currency-rate board for Turkish Lira (TRY).

## Users & need
Someone who checks the TRY value of major currencies many times a day. They want
the number instantly, in one glance, without menus, ads, or tapping. Read-only,
no login, no settings.

## Success
Open the app → read `1 USD = … TRY` and `1 EUR = … TRY` within a second. Values
stay fresh (10-minute refresh) and remain readable when offline.

## Scope
- One screen. No navigation, no accounts, no charts.
- Rates: TRY base; USD and EUR primary; GBP, CHF, JPY, AED, CAD secondary.
- Out of scope: conversion input, history, alerts, other base currencies.

## Assumptions (from the pinned brief)
- English-only labels ("1 USD =", "TRY").
- Rates are informational, not for trading decisions.
